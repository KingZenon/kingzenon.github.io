Kyoto, Japan

Ancient temples, traditional culture and peaceful gardens.

About This Destination

Kyoto is one of Japan's most historic cities, famous for its traditional temples, beautiful gardens and rich cultural heritage.

Visitors can explore ancient shrines, walk through peaceful bamboo forests and experience traditional Japanese architecture. Kyoto is also well known for its cherry blossoms in spring and colourful autumn leaves.

Top Experiences

  • Visit the famous Fushimi Inari Shrine
  • Explore the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
  • Discover the historic streets of Gion
  • Visit Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion
  • Experience a traditional Japanese tea ceremony

Travel Tips

  • Spring and autumn are popular seasons to visit.
  • Visit famous temples early to avoid crowds.
  • Use public transportation to explore the city.
  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking.
  • Respect local customs at shrines and temples.
