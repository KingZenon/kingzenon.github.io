Ha Long Bay, Vietnam

Emerald waters, limestone islands and unforgettable cruises.

About This Destination

Ha Long Bay is one of Vietnam's most famous natural attractions, known for its emerald-green waters, towering limestone islands and breathtaking scenery. It is also recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Visitors can explore the bay on scenic cruises, discover beautiful caves, kayak through peaceful waters and enjoy spectacular views of the surrounding islands. Ha Long Bay is an ideal destination for travelers who enjoy nature, relaxation and adventure.

Top Experiences

  • Enjoy a scenic cruise through Ha Long Bay
  • Explore the famous Sung Sot Cave
  • Go kayaking around limestone islands
  • Visit Ti Top Island for panoramic views
  • Watch the sunrise or sunset from a cruise boat

Travel Tips

  • October to April often offers comfortable sightseeing weather.
  • Book cruises in advance during popular travel periods.
  • Check weather conditions before boat trips.
  • Bring sunscreen, comfortable shoes and a light jacket.
  • Choose reputable cruise operators for safety and comfort.
