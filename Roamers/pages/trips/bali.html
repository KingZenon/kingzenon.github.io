Bali, Indonesia

Island escapes, nature and culture

About This Destination

Bali is one of Indonesia's most popular travel destinations, known for its tropical beaches, beautiful rice terraces, traditional temples and rich culture.

From the peaceful surroundings of Ubud to the coastal scenery of Uluwatu, Bali offers a combination of relaxation, adventure and cultural experiences.

Top Experiences

  • Explore the rice terraces and art scene in Ubud
  • Visit traditional Balinese temples
  • Relax on Bali's tropical beaches
  • Enjoy the sunset at Uluwatu
  • Discover local Balinese food and culture

Travel Tips

  • May to September is generally the drier season.
  • Respect local customs when visiting temples.
  • Allow extra travel time during busy traffic periods.
  • Bring sunscreen, light clothing and comfortable shoes.
