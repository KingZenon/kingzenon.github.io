Phuket, Thailand

Tropical beaches, island adventures and unforgettable sunsets.

About This Destination

Phuket is one of Thailand's most popular island destinations, famous for its beautiful beaches, crystal-clear waters and tropical scenery.

Visitors can explore nearby islands, enjoy water activities, discover local Thai culture and relax at stunning seaside resorts.

Top Experiences

  • Relax on Patong, Kata and Karon beaches
  • Take a boat trip to the Phi Phi Islands
  • Explore the colourful streets of Phuket Old Town
  • Visit the Big Buddha viewpoint when accessible
  • Watch the sunset at Promthep Cape

Travel Tips

  • November to April generally offers drier weather.
  • Book popular island tours in advance.
  • Use sunscreen and stay hydrated.
  • Check sea conditions before swimming or boating.
  • Respect local customs when visiting temples.
