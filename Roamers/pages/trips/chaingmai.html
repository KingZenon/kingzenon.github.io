Chiang Mai, Thailand

Mountain escapes, ancient temples and northern Thai culture.

About This Destination

Chiang Mai is a beautiful city in northern Thailand, known for its peaceful atmosphere, historic temples, green mountains and traditional Lanna culture.

Visitors can explore the ancient Old City, discover local markets, visit mountain viewpoints and enjoy northern Thai cuisine. Chiang Mai is a great destination for travelers who love nature, culture and adventure.

Top Experiences

  • Visit Wat Phra That Doi Suthep
  • Explore Chiang Mai's historic Old City
  • Discover Doi Inthanon National Park
  • Shop at the famous Sunday Walking Street Market
  • Experience northern Thai food and Lanna culture

Travel Tips

  • November to February usually offers cooler weather.
  • Check air quality during the burning season, often February to April.
  • Dress respectfully when visiting temples.
  • Bring comfortable shoes for walking and sightseeing.
  • Book popular mountain tours in advance.
